Unless the order expressly includes resale rights, the buyer may not sell, sublicense, publish, or redistribute the source code as a competing template, boilerplate, marketplace product, or downloadable codebase.

Hosting, domains, email providers, booking services, and other third-party tools are governed by their own terms and fees. The buyer is responsible for its accounts, credentials, content, consent language, privacy obligations, and lawful use.

## Warranty boundary

The seller will correct reproducible defects within the support period stated in the order when the product is used in the delivered environment. New features, provider changes, misuse, unauthorized modifications, and third-party outages are outside that warranty unless agreed separately.
