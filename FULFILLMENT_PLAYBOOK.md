# FollowDesk fulfilment playbook

## Product boundaries

This delivery is a focused lead follow-up and booking system for one business. It is not a custom enterprise CRM, marketing agency service, SMS platform, invoicing system, or unlimited integration engagement.

## Starter: 2 days

Included:

- Buyer branding and service list
- Included enquiry form
- Six-stage lead pipeline
- Immediate acknowledgement email
- Booking link
- Demo or buyer-provided deployment
- One revision

Workflow:

1. Confirm the questionnaire is complete.
2. Duplicate the clean repository and create the private order record.
3. Configure business details and step 1.
4. Deploy with demo email.
5. Run the QA checklist.
6. Receive buyer approval, then enable SMTP if included and credentials are ready.
7. Deliver the URL and operating guide.

## Standard: 4 days

Everything in Starter, plus:

- Four-step sequence
- Buyer booking provider
- Priorities, owner field, notes, filters, and CSV export
- Two revisions

## Advanced: 6 days

Everything in Standard, plus:

- White-label deployment on a buyer domain
- One webhook source or LeadDesk AI integration
- Delivery log review and conversion dashboard
- Technical documentation and self-hosting handoff
- Three revisions

## Scope controls

- One business and one brand per order.
- One language unless the package explicitly says otherwise.
- Buyer supplies approved copy, policy, sender domain, and booking link.
- Complex CRM migration, SMS, WhatsApp, invoicing, payments, and user roles require a separate quote.
- Revisions adjust included configuration and copy. They do not add new product modules.

## Safe order workflow

1. Keep email in demo mode while building.
2. Never copy production leads into a demo environment.
3. Ask the buyer to verify every message and link.
4. Send one controlled real-email test after sender approval.
5. Confirm suppression for booked, won, and lost leads.
6. Confirm database persistence after restart.
7. Complete the QA checklist before delivery.
