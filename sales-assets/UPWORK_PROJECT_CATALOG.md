## Project description

Stop losing enquiries because follow-up depends on memory, spreadsheets, or scattered inboxes.

I will customize and deploy FollowDesk, a focused lead follow-up and booking system for your service business. New enquiries enter a clear sales pipeline, receive approved branded emails, and get a direct path to book a call or appointment.

Depending on your package, your system can include:

- A branded website enquiry form
- New, contacted, qualified, booked, won, and lost stages
- Immediate confirmation and timed follow-up emails
- Calendly, Cal.com, or custom booking link
- Priorities, owners, internal notes, and activity history
- Search, filters, delivery log, and CSV export
- Editable brand settings and email templates
- Webhook capture from your website, automation tool, or LeadDesk AI
- Private dashboard and deployment documentation

The included demo email mode lets you review the complete workflow safely before real sending is enabled. Real email requires your approved sender identity and SMTP provider.

This project is designed for one business and one brand. It does not include a custom enterprise CRM, SMS or WhatsApp campaigns, invoicing, payments, data migration, or unlimited integrations unless we agree on them separately.

Send your brand, service list, approved messages, booking link, and deployment preference after ordering. I will configure, test, and deliver a focused system your team can actually use.

## FAQ

### Is this a subscription?

No. The project price covers the selected setup and delivery. The buyer pays any hosting, domain, email, booking, or third-party provider costs directly.

### Will it send real email immediately?

No. I deploy in safe demo mode first. Real sending is enabled only after you approve the templates and provide a verified sender through your email provider.

### Does it work with my existing website?

The included form can be used directly. Advanced includes one webhook connection from a compatible website or automation tool.

### Does it work with Calendly or Cal.com?

Yes. FollowDesk uses your approved scheduling URL in the confirmation screen and follow-up messages.

### Can it connect to LeadDesk AI?

Yes. The Advanced package can accept leads directly from LeadDesk AI through a protected webhook.

### Does it include SMS or WhatsApp?

No. Version 1 focuses on email follow-up and booking. SMS, WhatsApp, complex CRM migration, and custom integrations need a separate scope.

### Can my agency resell it?

Agency resale or redistribution rights are not included by default. Ask for a separate license and delivery scope.
